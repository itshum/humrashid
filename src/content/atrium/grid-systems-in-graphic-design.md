Müller-Brockmann was one of the architects of Swiss (International
Typographic) design, and this is his case for the grid as a discipline
rather than a constraint - a structure that makes a layout more
legible and more consistent, not less expressive, once you actually
learn to work within one.

Bilingual (English/German) and printed the way you'd expect a book
about grids to be printed - precise, restrained, no wasted space. It's
older than most of this list but the underlying logic is exactly what
still governs every responsive column system in use today.
