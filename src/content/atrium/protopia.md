Kelly coins "protopia" as an alternative to both utopia (arrival at
perfection) and dystopia (arrival at collapse) - a state of continuous,
incremental improvement where new problems are expected to show up
right alongside the solutions to the old ones.

It's a more honest and more useful lens for tracking progress than
either extreme, and a good corrective for anyone whose sense of the
future swings between utopian hype and doom.
