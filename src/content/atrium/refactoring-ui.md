Most design books stay conceptual. This one is almost entirely
tactical: use fewer font sizes than you think you need, don't rely on
gray text for hierarchy, add depth with layered shadows instead of
borders, that kind of thing. Each tip is small enough to apply in the
next five minutes.

Written by and for people who can already build a working interface
but keep landing on something that looks unfinished. It's the fastest
route from "functional" to "looks like a real designer touched it" I've
come across.
