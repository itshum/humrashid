Kelly wrote this for his birthday - one bit of advice for each year he'd
lived - and it reads like it: no hedging, no caveats, just the
compressed version of what he wishes someone had told him earlier.

Not every line lands the same for every reader, which is sort of the
point. It's a list built for returning to at different points in life,
not consuming once and filing away.
