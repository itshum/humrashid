Kelly's answer to the specific anxiety of feeling like you missed the
window - that the internet, or whatever the current wave is, already
had its founding moment and you weren't there for it.

His counter is that right now is always the earliest possible moment
relative to what's coming next, which is less a comforting platitude
than an argument backed by how early every previous wave actually was
when it looked already-obvious in hindsight.
