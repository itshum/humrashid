Structured like a field guide: 125 concepts, one per spread, each with
a short definition, an illustrated example, and a note on where the
idea originally came from (psychology, architecture, industrial
design, wherever). Hick's Law sits next to the Von Restorff effect
sits next to progressive disclosure.

Not a book you read start to finish so much as one you keep nearby and
pull off the shelf when you need the actual name for a principle
you've been applying by feel for years.
