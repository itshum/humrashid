This is the one you reach for after the shorter, friendlier books have
already made their case and you want the actual mechanics: how
personas are supposed to work, what goal-directed design means in
practice, and a long catalog of interaction patterns with the
reasoning behind each one.

Not a cover-to-cover read. It's a reference that rewards being opened
to a specific chapter when you're stuck on a specific problem - forms,
navigation, mobile gestures, whatever's in front of you that week.
