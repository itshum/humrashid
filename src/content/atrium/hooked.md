Eyal's Hook Model - trigger, action, variable reward, investment - is
a clean, mechanical description of how habit-forming products actually
work, and it's genuinely useful as a diagnostic tool once you see it.
You'll notice it everywhere afterward, including in your own phone.

Worth reading with a little skepticism. Eyal has walked back some of
the original framing over the years given how the same loop gets used
for attention-extraction as often as for something people are glad to
have a habit around. Read it for the model, argue with the ethics
yourself.
