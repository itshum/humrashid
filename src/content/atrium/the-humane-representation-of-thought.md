Originally a talk, transcribed with Victor's usual density of ideas
per paragraph. The argument: humans think with far more than words on
a screen - gesture, spatial memory, physical manipulation - and most
software has narrowed "thinking" down to typing and reading because
that's what's convenient to build, not because it's what's humane.

More provocation than practical guide, and better for it. It's the
kind of piece that reframes what you think the ceiling for software
even is, rather than telling you what to change tomorrow.
