Garrett's five-plane model - strategy, scope, structure, skeleton,
surface - is one of those diagrams that escaped the book it came from
and now shows up in decks everywhere without attribution. Reading the
original is worth it just to see the argument in full instead of the
compressed slide version.

Short and a little dated in its web-era examples, but the underlying
structure (each layer depends on the one below it, and skipping ahead
always costs you later) is still exactly how good product work gets
built.
