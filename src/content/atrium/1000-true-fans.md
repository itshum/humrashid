Written in 2008, well before Patreon or Substack existed, but it
predicted almost exactly the economy those platforms would end up
running on. The math is simple - 1,000 fans spending $100 a year is a
viable living - but the reframe, depth over reach, is the part that
actually changed how people think about audience.

Worth reading now specifically because the platforms it predicted now
exist, so you can check the theory against a decade and a half of real
evidence instead of taking it on faith.
