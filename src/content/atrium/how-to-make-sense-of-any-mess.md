Covert's definition of a "mess" is broad on purpose: a messy closet,
a confusing nav menu, an overloaded spreadsheet all count, and the
same basic moves (naming things clearly, grouping them sensibly,
deciding what to leave out) apply to all of them.

Written in plain, short sentences with almost no jargon, which makes
it the book I hand to someone who's never heard the term "information
architecture" but is drowning in one anyway.
