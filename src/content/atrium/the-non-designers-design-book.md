Robin Williams (not that one) built the whole book around four
principles - contrast, repetition, alignment, proximity, memorized
easily as C.R.A.P. - and then spends the rest of the pages showing the
same flyer or resume redesigned until each one clicks.

It's aimed at people with no design background at all, which is exactly
why it works as a gut check even if you have one. If you can't explain
why a layout is or isn't working using these four words, you probably
don't actually know why yet.
