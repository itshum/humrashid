Kholmatova frames a design system less as a Figma library and more as
a shared vocabulary a whole organization has to agree on and keep
maintaining - the patterns matter less than the process that keeps
them consistent as the team and product both grow.

Drawn from real case studies (Airbnb, Eurostar, and others), which
keeps it grounded instead of theoretical. Useful even if you'll never
build a formal system yourself, since most of the failure modes she
describes show up informally on any team past a certain size anyway.
