Victor's central claim is uncomfortable if you've spent your career on
interactivity: most software would be better as well-designed graphic
design - context-aware information display - than as something you
have to operate at all. Interaction should be the last resort, not the
default.

Long, dense, and worth every minute. It's less a set of tips and more
a full reframing of what "good software" is even trying to do, and it
changes how you look at dashboards, forms, and settings screens
afterward.
