Knapp lays out the design sprint hour by hour, day by day: Monday
mapping the problem, Tuesday sketching solutions, Wednesday deciding,
Thursday building a fake, Friday testing it on real people. It reads
like a recipe because it's meant to be run exactly as written the
first few times.

The specific five-day structure isn't sacred - most teams that use
this end up adapting it - but the underlying discipline (get to a
testable prototype before debating opinions any further) is the part
worth keeping.
