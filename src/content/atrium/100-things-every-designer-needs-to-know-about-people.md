Each of the hundred entries is two or three pages: a specific finding
from cognitive or perceptual psychology, the study behind it, and a
quick note on what it means for the thing you're designing. No grand
theory, just a large pile of well-sourced, individually useful facts.

Good as a reference to flip through rather than read straight, and a
solid way to back up a design decision with something more concrete
than "it felt right."
