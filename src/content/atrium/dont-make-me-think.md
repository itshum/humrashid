Krug's whole pitch fits in the title: if a user has to stop and think
about how something works, it's already broken. The book is short on
purpose - he says so himself - and every page is built around getting
you to actually go run a usability test instead of just reading about
one.

I hand this to anyone new to product or design before anything denser.
It's the fastest way to build the instinct that most usability
problems are obvious once you watch one real person get stuck.
