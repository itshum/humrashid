Dieter Rams wrote these ten principles in the 1970s while running
design at Braun, and nearly every "principles of good design" list
written since has been paraphrasing them without saying so. Reading
the original is short and worth doing just to see the source.

"Good design is as little design as possible" is the one that gets
quoted, but the whole list holds up - honest, unobtrusive,
understandable, long-lasting. Ten short lines that still function as
a genuine test for whether something is actually well designed.
