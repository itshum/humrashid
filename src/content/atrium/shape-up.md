Ryan Singer wrote up the actual process Basecamp uses to ship product
- six-week cycles, an "appetite" set before scoping instead of an
estimate after, and a betting table instead of an ever-growing
backlog - and Basecamp gave the whole thing away for free online
instead of selling it.

The appetite-over-estimate idea alone is worth the read: deciding how
much time a problem is worth before you scope the solution, rather
than scoping first and hoping the estimate comes in under budget.
