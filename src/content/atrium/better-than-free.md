Kelly's list of eight "generatives" - immediacy, personalization,
interpretation, authenticity, accessibility, embodiment, patronage,
findability - was written for the music and publishing industries
mid-collapse, but reads like a direct explanation of why people still
pay for things the internet makes free to copy.

The core move is reframing scarcity: once a copy is worthless, value
has to come from something that can't be copied at all.
