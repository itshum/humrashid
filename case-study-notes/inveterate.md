## What this case study is about

Inveterate is a membership/loyalty platform for eCommerce brands — instead of the usual points system, it packages perks (VIP discounts, early access, expedited shipping) into a paid membership, roughly an "Amazon Prime for any DTC brand" pitch. Nessa's engagement covered product design end to end: the merchant-facing platform (dashboard, membership builder, analytics), a full design system, and a brand identity refresh (external partners handled the actual brand identity work — Nessa built product on top of it).

## Scope of the work (paraphrased from the source, needs full rewrite for voice/structure)

1. **Membership platform core** — replacing points-based loyalty with a tiered membership model (VIP discounts, sneak peeks, exclusive drops, expedited shipping). Built a full global design system alongside it (reusable components, icon set, states) so the team could ship features faster.
2. **Brand identity** — Inveterate wanted a B2B product with consumer-brand personality, not typical stale SaaS. External brand partners led identity; Nessa carried that language across the product and into offline/marketing collateral (logo usage, out-of-home).
3. **Data dashboard** — a merchant-facing analytics view: signup dates, subscription spend, top-selling products, churn, AOV lift, at both a high level and a drill-down level.
4. **Membership program builder** — lets merchants configure their own program (tiered pricing, which benefits are active, custom landing pages), supporting both a white-glove launch path and a self-service path.
5. **Third-party integrations** — a dedicated tab for merchants to connect other apps into their membership program.
